About this tool

Water hardness and mineral data comes from laboratory analysis of real water samples — not AI estimates. Coverage includes 1,200+ US cities with verified PPM values sourced from utility Consumer Confidence Reports and EPA water quality databases.

Results reflect municipal tap water delivered to residential addresses. Private wells, whole-house softeners, and reverse osmosis systems will change your actual hardness levels.
