Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this AI Shaver Finder?
Our tool uses a logic model based on dermatological guidelines. It filters shavers based on their cutting mechanism (Foil vs. Rotary) to match your specific hair texture and skin sensitivity, ensuring a scientifically sound recommendation.
Should I choose a Foil or Rotary shaver?
If you have sensitive skin or need a very close shave on straight hair, a Foil Shaver (like Braun) is usually better. If you have curly hair or shave irregular contours (like a head), a Rotary Shaver (like Philips) might be preferable. Our tool decides this for you.
Does expensive mean better for shavers?
Not always. High-end shavers often have "cleaning stations" and digital displays which add cost but don't change the shave quality. However, very cheap models often lack powerful motors needed for thick hair. Our "Value" option finds the sweet spot.
