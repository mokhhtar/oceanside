About Us & Editorial Policy

Who we are, how we research, and how we're funded.

What Oceanside Hair Salon Is

Despite the name, we are not a hair salon. Oceanside Hair Salon is an independent, informational website covering hair care, grooming, and local salon/barber options in Oceanside, CA and North County San Diego. We do not operate a physical location, offer hair services, or employ stylists.

How We Research and Write

Articles on this site are drafted with the help of AI tools and reviewed by an editor before publishing. For local business roundups (salons, barbershops), we verify names, addresses, and service details against public sources at the time of writing.

Prices, hours, and business details change over time and can vary by location. Always confirm current pricing and availability directly with the business before visiting.

If you spot outdated or incorrect information, please let us know and we'll correct it.

Affiliate Disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click a link and purchase a recommended product, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This does not influence which products or businesses we choose to cover.
