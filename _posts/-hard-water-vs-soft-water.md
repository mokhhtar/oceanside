## How Hard Water Affects Hair: The "Dry but Greasy" Problem

Hard water creates a distinctive pattern of damage that worsens progressively over time. The minerals in hard water don't just rinse away—they bond chemically to the keratin proteins in your hair shaft.

## How to Adjust Your Hair Routine Based on Your Water Type

### For Hard Water Hair Care
1. **Prioritize chelating treatments:** Use shampoos containing EDTA or citric acid.
2. **Clarify regularly:** To remove mineral and product buildup.
3. **Deep condition consistently:** Because hard water blocks moisture.

### For Soft Water Hair Care
1. **Reduce shampoo quantity:** Start with half the amount you used before.
2. **Extend rinse time:** Learn to trust the slippery feeling.
3. **Switch to lightweight products:** Avoid heavy oils that weigh hair down.
