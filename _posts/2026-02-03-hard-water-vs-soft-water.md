### Introduction
You finally installed a water softener after years of struggling with dull, coated hair. The anticipation builds as you step into the shower for that first wash with soft water. You lather up, expecting luxurious, clean hair—and instead, your hair feels strangely slippery, almost slimy.

The truth is more nuanced than "hard water bad, soft water good." Each water type creates distinct challenges for hair.

### Common Mistakes That Make It Worse
The instinctive responses to soft water sensations often compound the problems:

* **Using more shampoo:** You don't need more product—you need less.
* **Rinsing excessively:** Learn to trust that the slippery feeling means clean.
* **Heavy products:** Rich conditioners formulated for hard water become overwhelming in soft water.
